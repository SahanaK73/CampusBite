About CampusBite

CampusBite is a campus food-ordering platform designed to help students save time by ordering food in advance and collecting it from their college canteens.

The problem

Students often have to wait in long queues and may not know whether food is available.

The solution

CampusBite allows students to:

  • Browse menus
  • Search for food
  • Check availability
  • Place orders
  • Track order status
  • Pick up food using a pickup code
